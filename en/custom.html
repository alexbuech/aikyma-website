The pattern we see

PoC is easy. Production is hard.

Mid-sized companies today can build their first AI PoCs on their own. What's regularly missing is the ability to turn a working PoC into a productive, broadly usable operation — sustainably.

1
Operations know-how is scarce

Experience in safely running, monitoring and continuously developing AI solutions is rare in mid-sized companies. Developers aren't automatically operations teams.

2
Resources dry up after the PoC

Time and personnel for operations, monitoring and development are typically depleted after a PoC. The solution stalls before it can scale.

3
Standards move fast

What qualifies as a proven pattern today will be outdated in six months. Without a methodical foundation, an AI solution gets stuck in its year of development.

4
Integration stays shallow

PoCs often work with CSV exports and manual data handovers. Deep, bidirectional integration into ERP, CRM and PIM — the actual production stage — remains an open construction site.

A PoC shows feasibility. Production shows impact. Between the two lies the work we take on.
— aikyma team
Framework capabilities

What our framework delivers.

The foundation that turns an AI PoC into a reliable, multi-tenant, auditable operation. Nine building blocks — vertical-independent, production-proven.

⚡
ERP / CRM / PIM Integration

Production-proven connectors to Business Central, SAP (incl. IDoc), industry ERPs as well as CRM and PIM systems. Bidirectional data flows, no silos.

◫
Multi-tenant Runtime

One image, one container per tenant. Production-proven — including hardened setup in third-party Azure.

◆
Plugin Architecture

Customer-specific logic as an isolated plugin — without touching the core. Fault-tolerant, cleanly versioned.

🔒
Secret Management

Encrypted configuration per tenant, optionally with Azure Key Vault. Credentials never in code, never in the image.

↻
State Machine & HITL

Every transaction is traceable. Resume exactly where it broke after a crash. Structured review instead of inbox chaos.

💬
Dialog Agent Pattern

Autonomous AI precisely where it belongs — at the human-dialog boundary. Never on safety-critical transactions.

◉
Admin Server & Roles

Three server modes (Full / Control Plane / Tenant), role and permission model, CLI for operations.

▲
Governed Learning

Rule engine, teach-test-confirm, layered memory. AI learns — but in a controlled and traceable way.

⇄
Pluggable Connectors

Email, LLM, database — all selectable via configuration, not through code forks. No vendor lock-in.

What this means in practice: You don't have to invent multi-tenancy, HITL workflows, secret management, audit trails, container deployment, dialog patterns, or deep integration into your target systems. These production-proven building blocks are available from day one. Your investment flows into what actually creates value for you — the part of the solution tailored to your process.
